At 0 policies, Calico and Cilium will have similar latency, maybe Cilium slightly higher because of its eBPF programs fixed cost. As I add policies (10 -> 1000), Calico’s latency will grow, because each packet is checked against a longer iptables list. Cilium’s will stay almost flat, because eBPF looks rules up in a table. At 1000 policies I expect Calico's p99 latency to be at least 2× Cilium's
